FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions students and parents ask.

Do you teach students outside Kenya?

Yes. Excel Tuition Centre is designed for online learning, allowing students to access tuition from different countries and time zones.

Which curricula do you support?

We support major international pathways including Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA International, IB and selected UK examination boards.

Do you offer SAT preparation?

Yes. We offer SAT preparation focused on the skills and question types required for the examination.

Do you offer IELTS preparation?

Yes. We provide preparation support for IELTS, including reading, writing, listening and speaking skills.

Can I request a specific subject?

Yes. Contact us with the subject, academic level, curriculum or exam and your learning goals.
